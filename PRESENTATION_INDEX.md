## 📞 Quick Troubleshooting

**"I'm nervous"**
→ That's normal! Read the confidence guide section. Everyone gets nervous.

**"I don't know what to say"**
→ That's why you have the script. Read it until it feels natural.

**"My accent/voice sounds weird"**
→ Everyone feels this way. You sound fine. Viewers care about content, not accent.

**"I keep messing up the same part"**
→ Practice that section separately. Record just that part multiple times.

**"My video is too long"**
→ Cut pauses, speak slightly faster, or trim the demo time.

**"I don't sound confident"**
→ Slow down. Pause between sentences. Speak from the chest, not your throat.
